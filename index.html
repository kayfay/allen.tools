03 / Flagship Case Study

Yellow Rose IMS: Physical Yields Meet Edge AI Vision

Barbecue is a brutal business of razor-thin margins and physical physics. Raw beef brisket shrinks between 44% and 52% during a 14-hour smoke cycle. Standard inventory management software assumes a 1:1 recipe conversion, leaving operators blind to true meat yields, food costs, and shrinkage.

Rather than paying thousands of dollars for clunky enterprise restaurant SaaS that kitchen staff refuse to use, I designed and built the Yellow Rose Inventory Management System from the ground up.

System Architecture • Edge Intelligence Pipeline
STEP 01
Multimodal Camera
Staff snap a shelf photo of Cryovac cases inside the walk-in cooler.
STEP 02
Cloudflare Worker
Edge middleware processes the payload with zero cold starts.
STEP 03
Gemini Vision AI
Gemini 1.5 Flash extracts cuts, batch tags, and counts via structured JSON.
STEP 04
Yield Reconciliation
Clover POS sales match against physical smoker yields in real time.

Key Engineering Breakthroughs

  • Multimodal Shelf Vision: Kitchen staff capture a walk-in storage photo. The image routes via a lightweight Cloudflare Worker to gemini-1.5-flash, returning structured JSON counts of briskets, pork butts, and soda bibs—eliminating manual clipboard counts.
  • Real-Time Yield Reconciliation: A custom Python depletion engine compares Clover POS register receipts against raw butcher weights and physical cook yields, isolating kitchen trimming loss, smoker shrinkage, and unaccounted variance.
  • Two-Way Edge Sync: High-speed local SQLite caching paired with bi-directional Google Sheets API synchronization delivers live cost-of-goods figures to floor managers without software training overhead.
