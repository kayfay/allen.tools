# Design System: Allen Tools Redesign
 
 ## Core Directives & Aesthetics
 - **Framework:** Tailwind CSS via CDN 
 - **Inspiration:** Clean, Modern Tech Portfolio (Soft elegance, breathable whitespace, subtle depth)
 - **Vibe:** Professional, approachable, and authoritative. Moving away from aggressive brutalism toward a refined, easy-on-the-eyes interface.
 
 ## Typography
 - **Primary Font:** `Inter`, sans-serif (Clean, highly legible, modern tech standard)
 - *Note:* Headers should utilize `font-semibold` or `font-semibold tracking-tight` rather than heavy bold weights to maintain elegance.
 
 ## Colors (Machine-Readable Constraints)
 - **Primary Color (Accent):** `#475569` (Slate 600 - A sophisticated, muted blue-gray used for primary actions and active states)
 - **Secondary Color (Text/Borders):** `#334155` (Slate 700 - Soft Charcoal, replacing pure black to reduce eye strain)
 - **Background Color:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50 - A very faint cool gray for the page background)
 - **Surface Color:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White - Used for content cards and the sidebar to create subtle layering)
 - **Subtle Accent:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200 - Used for delicate 1px borders and dividers)
 
 ## Spacing & Shadows 
 - **Borders:** Use delicate `1px` borders (`border-slate-200`) instead of heavy brutalist blocks.
 - **Shadows:** Use soft, diffused drop shadows (`shadow-sm` or `shadow-md`) to lift interactive elements and surface cards, rather than solid hard shadows.
 - **Rounding:** Use subtle rounded corners (`rounded-lg` or `rounded-xl`) to soften the edges of the UI.
 - **Spacing:** Maintain ample whitespace, but reduce extreme brutalist margins to standard, comfortable reading rhythms.
 
 ## UX & Intent
 - **User Context:** Hiring managers, technical recruiters, or executives looking for quick validation of the consultant's AI/Data Science skills.
 - **Core Action:** Clicking the "Schedule a Consultation" link. 
 - **Application:** The design should feel frictionless. Navigation should be subtle but clear. Call-to-actions should be inviting, using the primary Slate color with soft hover transitions, rather than screaming for attention.
